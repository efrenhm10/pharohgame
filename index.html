Horus in the Flesh A political simulation of the Black Land
How to rule

Each season you receive a Strategic Briefing: the state of the realm, then either a Crisis or an Opportunity. Seal one decree, read what the chronicle records, then let the season turn.

  • Ma’at is your legitimacy as a god. Floods, defeats and hunger break it.
  • Grain pays everyone at home. Gold is for foreign kings.
  • Four power blocks, the Priesthood, Bureaucracy, Military and Harem, turn dangerous as their approval falls.
  • Storing grain in good years unlocks Prepared options in bad ones.
  • Keys: 1–4 seal a decree, Enter turns the season.

Your reign is saved in this browser automatically.
