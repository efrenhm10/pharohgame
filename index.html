How to rule

Each season begins in the Throne Room with a Strategic Briefing: a Crisis or an Opportunity. You must seal one decree before the season can end.

Around it, the desks let you rule directly: rations and taxes, troops and commanders, your wives and every child’s schooling and marriage, officials, nomes and foreign kings.

  • Orders cost Royal Attention ⚡, about 3 a season. Standing orders (rations, taxes, palace) are free.
  • Ma’at is your legitimacy as a god. Grain pays everyone at home; gold is for foreign kings.
  • Storing grain in good years unlocks Prepared options in bad ones.
  • Keys: 1–4 seal a decree, Enter ends the season.

Your reign is saved in this browser automatically.

Abandon this reign and begin again in Year 9? Your current reign will be lost.
